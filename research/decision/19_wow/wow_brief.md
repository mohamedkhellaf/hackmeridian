# WOW-FIRST brief

## The situation

- **Event:** HackMeridian 2026, Lisbon. Stellar blockchain, Genesis category (new idea → working MVP).
- **Deadline:** Monday 12:00. It is Sunday ~15:00.
- **Team:** 3 developers, plus an AI that writes most of the code, so ambitious builds are OK.

The team rejected the earlier ideas as **"neither novel enough nor interesting enough."** Earlier rounds produced: a scam undo-wallet, a B2B post-dated cheque on pre-signed transactions, debt netting, witness tokens, café FX liquidity, and BNPL. They were sensible and evidence-backed but **boring**. Do not repeat that.

## What the judges explicitly said they want

From the official "Judging Insights" session:
- "Bring your craziest ideas."
- "The off-the-wall kind of weird ones … human creativity … something you've put your fingerprints on" (Elliot, SDF). He cited an NFT-battle game built on verifiable randomness as the most memorable winner.
- "Creative things with AI agents rather than a similar project over and over again" (Kaan, SDF).
- Stellar Unlocked = "building something that used to not be possible on Stellar … what you didn't have the ability to do in the last hackathon."
  - New 2025–2026 capabilities: BN254 and Poseidon/Poseidon2 host functions (ZK verifiers, Protocol 25–26), BLS12-381, confidential tokens (OpenZeppelin, developer preview), Stellar Private Payments (testnet), x402/MPP agent payments, smart accounts with passkeys and policies, CCTP/Axelar bridging on testnet.
  - Classic primitives used weirdly: the DEX, path payments, claimable balances with predicates, hash(x) signers, pre-auth transactions, sequence numbers and time bounds, asset auth flags and clawback, sponsored reserves.
- **Official idea areas:** "Protocol-powered games: real onchain stakes and outcomes on core protocol features"; "ZK, natively"; "Old-school assets, new tricks"; "Bring users and liquidity to Stellar".
- **Still required:**
  - a real reason someone would use it, even if the reason is fun, culture or delight;
  - Stellar must be essential;
  - one hero flow must work end to end on testnet by tomorrow;
  - "my mom can understand it" helps.

## Ideas that are already taken (other teams' AIs produce these, so avoid them)

- agent wallets with spend limits / x402 pay-per-API
- scam undo wallets, social recovery, inheritance
- remittance, cash links
- savings circles, split bills
- escrow of any kind, ticket anti-scalping, loyalty points, crowdfunding
- streaming payroll, invoice factoring, debt netting
- generic ZK private payroll or voting
- RWA tokenization, carbon credits, DAO voting

## What to produce

**3 ideas that make a judge say "wait, what? … that's brilliant"** and that they'd still be talking about at dinner.

For each:
- a hook (one sentence);
- why it's delightful or surprising;
- who would actually use it and why;
- the exact Stellar mechanism (verify protocol claims against developers.stellar.org or the stellar-raven MCP if unsure);
- why it's "only on Stellar" or "newly possible";
- the 90-second demo and its jaw-drop moment;
- what's hard to build by tomorrow (be honest);
- a prior-art check: search Scout via stellar-raven for prior Stellar hackathon builds, and do a quick web check.

**Rank them by wow × feasibility.** Interesting beats safe, but a broken demo scores zero. Time-box: **15 minutes**. Be bold.
